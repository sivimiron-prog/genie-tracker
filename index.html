Tilt comes from the accelerometer and is accurate (±0.2–0.5°). Direction comes from the compass and is the weak point: ±2–5°, and the motor's magnet, tripod and steel bolts pull it off. For the best result use the landmark method: hold the phone flat, away from the tracker and tripod, turn until the compass shows true north (0°), pick a distant tree or light in that direction, then aim the tracker at it by eye.

How good is good enough?

1 · Calibrate (once)

  1. Put a piece of tape across the rotating plate and the base so they line up.
  2. Tap Start calibration. The plate turns at full speed (~35 s per turn).
  3. Let it pass the mark 1–3 times, then tap Stop the moment the tape lines up again.
  4. Set how many full turns it made. 3 turns is 3× more accurate.

2 · Direction check

Daytime test: aim the camera at a distant object, start Stars and watch for 2 minutes. The view should slowly drift the way the sky moves (east → west, like the Sun). If it goes the other way, flip it here.
